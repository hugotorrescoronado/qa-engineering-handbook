# Quality Metrics

## Defect Density

Defects per story, feature, or release.

---

## Escaped Defects

Defects found in production.

---

## Automation Coverage

Automated tests divided by total tests.

---

## Pass Rate

Passed Tests / Executed Tests

---

## MTTD

Mean Time To Detect

---

## MTTR

Mean Time To Resolve

---

## Example Dashboard

| Metric | Goal |
|----------|----------|
| Pass Rate | >95% |
| Escaped Defects | <2 |
| Automation Coverage | >80% |
| MTTR | <24h |
